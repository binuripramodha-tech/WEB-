Kyoto, Japan

Why I Want to Visit

Kyoto is full of historic temples, traditional gardens, and quiet streets that still feel like stepping back in time.

How I Would Plan My Trip

I would spend a week exploring the city on foot and by train, staying close to the old town.

Places to See

The Fushimi Inari Shrine is a must-see, along with the bamboo groves of Arashiyama.

Local Food to Try

Matcha sweets and fresh soba noodles are two dishes I would want to try.

A Travel Tip

Visit in early spring to catch the cherry blossoms in bloom.
